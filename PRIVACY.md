# Privacy Notice — IntelliFIR

**Last updated**: November 2026

---

## What IntelliFIR Processes

IntelliFIR processes citizen complaint information to generate draft First Information Reports (FIRs). This may include:

- Personal names (complainants, witnesses, reported persons)
- Locations and addresses
- Incident dates and times
- Contact information
- Descriptions of alleged incidents

---

## How Data Is Used

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Complaint text | NER extraction, FIR draft generation, validation |
| Uploaded documents | Text extraction and storage |
| User accounts | Authentication and audit trail |
| Extracted entities | Structured case representation |

---

## Data Storage

- All data is stored in a local PostgreSQL database
- Uploaded files are stored locally with UUID-based filenames
- Semantic embeddings are stored in a local FAISS index
- **No complaint data is sent to external services** unless an external LLM provider is explicitly configured

---

## LLM and External Services

By default (`LLM_PROVIDER=none`), IntelliFIR does not send complaint data to any external AI service.

If `LLM_PROVIDER=ollama` is configured, complaint-derived facts are sent to a **locally running** Ollama server. No data leaves the deployment host.

If you configure a hosted LLM provider in a custom deployment, review that provider's data retention and privacy policies before processing real complaint data.

---

## Access Control

- Role-based access restricts complaint records to authorized users
- Complaint documents are not accessible via public URLs
- All significant actions are recorded in an audit log

---

## AI-Generated Content

All FIR drafts generated by IntelliFIR are:
- Clearly marked as "AI-GENERATED DRAFT — REQUIRES AUTHORIZED HUMAN REVIEW"
- Not intended for direct official submission
- Subject to authorized human review before any official action

---

## Disclaimer

IntelliFIR does not autonomously determine guilt, make final legal decisions, or officially register FIRs. All outputs require review by authorized personnel.

The accuracy of AI-generated extractions and drafts depends on the quality of the input complaint and the configured models. Users are responsible for verifying all AI-generated content.
